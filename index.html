Which should I use: the .zip or a folder of GPX files?

Apple Health export.zip (Health app → profile picture → Export All Health Data) is the richest option: it carries the real activity type, Apple's distance and the exact local time zone of every workout. Reading it takes a minute or two because the zip holds a multi-gigabyte export.xml that has to be scanned; the page does this in a stream so memory stays small.

A folder of GPX files (for example the workout-routes folder from an unzipped export, or a Strava / Garmin / Komoot bulk export) loads in seconds. GPX files carry no workout metadata, so the activity is taken from the file when it says so and otherwise guessed from average speed, and local time is estimated from longitude. If you pick an unzipped apple_health_export folder, the page also reads its export.xml and you get the full detail.

Location history (.json) is your Google Timeline export (the location-history.json you get from the Maps app on your phone). It captures where you were outside workouts: each trip becomes a route coloured by mode (walking, cycling, car, transit, flight), each place you stayed becomes a point, and sparse location traces are kept as thin lines. Trips are straight lines from start to end because the export doesn't store the road taken. Place visits are hidden by default; tick “Visit” under Activity, or set Heat counts to “Time spent”, to see where your hours go. Load it as its own source so it stays separate from your workouts.

To compare two people or two periods, import each as its own named source, then use the Compare tab.
